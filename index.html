What is a Cube?

A cube is a three-dimensional solid object bounded by six square faces, with three faces meeting at each vertex.

  • ✨ 6 Congruent Square Faces
  • ✨ 12 Edges (All equal in length)
  • ✨ 8 Vertices (Corners)
  • ✨ All angles are 90°
Volume = $s^3$  |  Surface Area = $6s^2$
📦

What is a Cuboid?

A cuboid (rectangular prism) is a 3D box shape bounded by six rectangular faces.

  • ✨ 6 Rectangular Faces
  • ✨ 12 Edges (3 sets of 4 parallel edges: $l, w, h$)
  • ✨ 8 Vertices (Corners)
  • ✨ Opposite faces are identical
Volume = $l \cdot w \cdot h$  |  $SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)$

📊 Cube vs. Cuboid Quick Comparison

Property Cube 🧊 Cuboid 📦
Face Shape All 6 faces are identical Squares 6 Rectangles (Opposite pairs identical)
Edge Lengths All 12 edges are EQUAL ($s$) Edges vary in Length ($l$), Width ($w$), Height ($h$)
Volume Formula $V = s^3$ $V = l \times w \times h$
Surface Area Formula $SA = 6s^2$ $SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)$
